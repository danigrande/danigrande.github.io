Who Am I

I'm a Principal Product Manager with a builder's mindset. I don't stop at the spec — I use AI to take products from strategy to working software: requirements, architecture, code, tests, and evaluation.

To prove it, I ran a solo experiment: could one PM, working with AI agents, ship a real ERP? The result is GrandeSuite Next — a financial platform for growing businesses, built end-to-end with agentic coding and the BMAD method, and still in progress.

Alongside it, I built a multi-personality AI chatbot inside a World Cup prediction pool app (live as a prototype), created agent workflows with LangFlow — including a feedback-to-PRD pipeline — and wrote a six-module product strategy for an AI product, from the bet to the pitch.

I'm looking for a product role where AI-assisted development is at the core: building software, not just documenting it.
