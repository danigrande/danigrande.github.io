Who Am I

I'm a Principal Product Manager with a builder's mindset. I don't stop at the spec — I use AI to take products from strategy to working software: requirements, architecture, code, tests, and evaluation.

I spent five years as a Principal Product Manager at Oracle NetSuite, working on the ERP — where I saw first-hand what it takes, and what it costs, to ship an enterprise product. That experience is the direct motivation behind my work now.

To prove what's possible, I ran a solo experiment: could one PM, working with AI agents, ship a real ERP? The result is GrandeSuite Next — built end-to-end with agentic coding and the BMAD method, and still in progress.

Alongside it, I built a multi-personality AI chatbot inside a World Cup prediction pool app (live as a prototype), created agent workflows with LangFlow — including a feedback-to-PRD pipeline — and wrote a six-module AI product strategy framework, from the bet to the pitch.

I'm looking for a senior product role where AI-assisted development is at the core — Principal PM for an AI product, or Head of Product for an AI-first company. Building software, not just documenting it.
